Title: Attention and Impulsivity in Child: A Case Study

Clinical Summary: The client, a 7-year-old child with attention deficit hyperactivity disorder (ADHD) and impulsivity, was referred for concerns related to academics and executive functioning. The parent reports difficulty sustaining attention during classroom tasks and frequent distractibility at home, while the teacher observation indicates inconsistent task completion and challenges with following multi-step instructions. Behavioral rating scales completed by both parents and teachers indicate elevated scores in areas of inattention and executive functioning.

Purpose: This assessment provides a comprehensive overview of the child's clinical presentation, including their diagnosis, treatment plan, and ongoing care. It also includes an analysis of relevant cognitive and attentional-related domains, and identifies areas for further intervention. The summary is written in clear and concise language with objective language and clinically appropriate terminology, to ensure accuracy and clarity in the communication of findings.
