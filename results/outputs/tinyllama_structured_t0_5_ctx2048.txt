Title: Attention, Impulsivity, and Academic Performance in a 7-Year-Old Child Referred for Concerns

Introduction:
The client is a 7-year-old child referred for concerns related to attention, impulsivity, and academic performance. The parent reports difficulty sustaining attention during classroom tasks and frequent distractibility at home. The teacher has observed inconsistent task completion and chaos with following multi-step instructions. Behavioral rating scales completed by both parents and teachers indicate elevated scores in the areas of inattention and executive functioning, particularly in verbal comprehension. Cognitive testing revealed overall average intellectual functioning, with strengths noted in verbal comprehension, while working memory and processing speed scores were comparatively weaker. Emotional and behavioral concerns were reported beyond attention-related difficulties. The child demonstrates age-appropriate social engagement and communication skills.

Assessment:
1. Attention:
a. Cognitive testing revealed overall average intellectual functioning, with strengths noted in verbal comprehension.
b. Behavioral rating scales completed by both parents and teachers indicate elevated scores in the areas of inattention and executive functioning, particularly in verbal comprehension.
c. Cognitive tests revealed overall average intelligence, with strengths noted in verbal comprehension.
d. Social engagement and communication skills are age-appropriate.

2. Impulsivity:
a. Emotional and behavioral concerns were reported beyond attention-related difficulties.
b. The child demonstrates age-appropriate social engagement and communication skills.

3. Academic performance:
a. Cognitive testing revealed overall average intelligence, with strengths noted in verbal comprehension.
b. Social engagement and communication skills are age-appropriate.

Conclusion:
Based on the assessment results, it can be inferred that the client has attention-related difficulties, including inattention and executive functioning, as well as impulsivity and academic performance concerns. The child demonstrates age-appropriate social engagement and communication skills, indicating that these areas are not impacted by the client's issues. However, further diagnostic testing may be necessary to fully understand the extent of the client's difficulties.
