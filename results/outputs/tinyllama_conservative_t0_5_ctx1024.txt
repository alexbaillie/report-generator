The client is a 7-year-old child referred for concerns related to attention, impulsivity, and academic performance. Parent reports difficulty sustaining attention during classroom tasks and frequent distractibility at home, while teacher observations indicate inconsistent task completion and chaos with following multi-step instructions. Behavioral rating scales completed by both parents and teachers indicate elevated scores in the areas of inattention and executive functioning, but notably weaker scores in verbal comprehension and processing speed. The child demonstrates age-appropriate social engagement and communication skills.
