Title: Relative Weaknesses in Working Memory Impact Academic Functioning in a 9-Year-Old Child Referred for Concerns about Attention and Academic Performance

Introduction:
The child referred to the clinic has been exhibiting difficulties sustaining attention and completing tasks. The clinician has noted that the child's teacher reports difficulty maintaining focus on tasks, leading to reduced academic performance. The child's test results indicate a weakness in working memory (VCI=92, WMi=78), which may impact their academic functioning.

Cognitive Function:
The child's cognitive functioning is evaluated using the WISC-V assessment. The VCI score indicates that the child has moderate difficulties with sustaining attention and completing tasks. The WMI score indicates a weakness in working memory, which may impact academic performance.

Attention:
The child's attention is evaluated through the PSI test. The results indicate that the child has moderate difficulties with attending to task-related stimuli. This suggests that they may have difficulty focusing on tasks and maintaining their attention over time.

Functional Status:
The child's functional status is assessed using the FSIQ test. The results indicate that the child's cognitive functioning is moderate, indicating that they are able to perform basic tasks but may require support in areas of difficulty.

Conclusion:
The child's weaknesses in working memory and attention may impact their academic performance. The clinician recommends further evaluation with a neuropsychologist to determine the specific areas of concern and develop appropriate interventions.
