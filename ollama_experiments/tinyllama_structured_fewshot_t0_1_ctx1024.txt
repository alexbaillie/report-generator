Title: Child with Relative Weaknesses in Working Memory Impacting Academic Functioning

INTRODUCTION:
The input report summarizes the child's intake notes, which indicate concerns about attention and academic performance. The teacher has reported difficulty sustaining attention and completing tasks. Test results show that the WISE-V (Working Memory Impairment Scale) score is 92, with a WMi (Working Memory Impairment Index) of 78, and PSI (Performance on Sleepiness Indices) of 85. The FSIQ (Fitness for School Index) is also high at 88. Based on these test results, the child's cognitive functioning may be impacted by a relative weakness in working memory. This report provides a summary of the child's academic and attentional performance to help clinicians better understand their needs and develop appropriate interventions.
