Title: Child with Relative Weaknesses in Working Memory Impacting Academic Functioning

Introduction:
The child referred for evaluation is a 9-year-old boy who has been experiencing difficulties sustaining attention and completing tasks. The teacher reported that the child's attentional capacity was impaired, with difficulty maintaining focus on tasks for extended periods of time. The WISC-V test results showed that the child had a VCI of 92, a WMi of 78, a PSI of 85, and an FSIQ of 88.

Cognitive Functions:
The child's cognitive functioning is being impacted by his relative weaknesses in working memory. This means that he has difficulty sustaining attention for extended periods of time, which can lead to difficulties completing tasks or maintaining focus on tasks. The WISC-V test results suggest that the child may have a VCI of 92, indicating that he has some deficits in working memory.

Attention and Attentional Control:
The teacher reported that the child's attention was often disrupted by distractions or interruptions, which can lead to difficulty maintaining focus on tasks for extended periods of time. The WISC-V test results suggest that the child may have some deficits in attention and attentional control, with a PSI of 85 indicating that he has some difficulties regulating his attention and focusing on tasks.

Academic Functioning:
The child's academic functioning is also being impacted by his relative weaknesses in working memory. This means that he may struggle to complete tasks or maintain focus on tasks, which can affect his overall academic performance. The WISC-V test results suggest that the child may have a WMi of 78, indicating that he has some deficits in working memory.

Conclusion:
In summary, the child's cognitive and attention-related functions are being impacted by his relative weaknesses in working memory. This can lead to difficulties sustaining attention for extended periods of time, which can affect academic functioning. The WISC-V test results suggest that the child may have some deficits in working memory, with a VCI of 92 indicating that he has some difficulties in maintaining focus on tasks for extended periods of time.
