Psychological Evaluation Report Summary for the Child Referred Due to Concerns about Attention and Academic Performance

Introduction:
The child referred is a 9-year-old boy with concerns about attention and academic performance. The child has been experiencing difficulty sustaining attention and completing tasks, leading to difficulties in achieving academic goals. The teacher's report indicates that the child exhibits weaknesses in working memory, inhibition, and executive functioning.

Cognitive Assessment:
The WISC-V test results indicate a significant deficit in working memory (VCI = 92), attention (WMI = 78), perception of self (PSI = 85), and processing information (FSIQ = 88). These scores suggest that the child has difficulties maintaining attention, completing tasks efficiently, and regulating emotions.

Attention-Related Assessments:
The WISC-V test results also indicate a significant deficit in attentional control (VCI = 92), executive functioning (WMI = 78), and working memory (PSI = 85). These findings suggest that the child has difficulty regulating attention, completing tasks efficiently, and managing emotions.

Based on the provided data, it is clear that the child has significant cognitive and attentional deficits.
