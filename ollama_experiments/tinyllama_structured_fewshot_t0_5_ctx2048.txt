Introduction:
The input report summarizes the child's initial assessment based on their performance on the WISE-V test. Academic Functioning:
The PSI and FSIQ scores also indicate that the child has academic problems, which may contribute to their weaknesses in working memory. The report notes that the child's academic performance may be affected by these weaknesses as well.
